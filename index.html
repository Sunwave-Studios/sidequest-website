SideQuest

One small weird thing to do today. One bigger quest a week. Local-only, on your device — no accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Privacy

Privacy policy

SideQuest does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create — your profile name, completed quests, streaks, theme and font preferences — stays in your device's local storage. Nothing is ever sent off-device.

We do not use analytics, ad networks, crash reporting, or any third-party services. There is no account on a server because there is no server. Notifications are scheduled and fired entirely on your device by the operating system.

If you uninstall the app or tap Delete account & data in Settings, all of your data is permanently removed from your device. Because we never had a copy, there is no further deletion request required from us.

Children under 13: SideQuest is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.
