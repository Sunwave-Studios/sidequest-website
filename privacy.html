Privacy Policy — Sidequest

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Summary

  • Sidequest is a local-first app. Data stays on your device by default.
  • Optional cloud sync and account features use a Supabase backend controlled by the developer; cloud sync runs only if you create or sign into an account.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or analytics providers by default.

2. What data we collect

Local-only data (default)

  • Profile fields you enter locally (display name).
  • Quest data stored locally: saved quests, completions, skipped/finished state, preferences, and lightweight key/value settings.
  • App preferences such as theme, typography, and notification toggles.
  • Device-local identifiers created by the app for internal use only (not transmitted unless you opt in to cloud sync).

Optional cloud data (only if you sign in / opt into sync)

  • Account identifier and email used for authentication.
  • Profile information you explicitly save to your account.
  • Quest completions, group membership, and other app content you choose to sync.
  • Notification preferences if the app is configured to persist them server-side.

3. How we use your data

Local storage: to provide the app experience (daily quests, history, preferences). Cloud sync (optional): to back up and share your data across devices when you sign in. Notifications: if you enable scheduled reminders, the app schedules them with the operating system; the app may persist notification preferences locally or server-side depending on configuration.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. We do not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs by default. If you opt into cloud features, your data is stored on the developer’s Supabase backend (or another backend you configure). That backend is subject to its own policies and security controls. The app may rely on OS-level services (e.g., local notifications), which are governed by the OS vendor.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or use the app’s delete functionality. If you create an account and then delete it through the app, the app will attempt to remove your server-side data; deletion requests may take effect after backend processing and cannot be undone.

6. Security

The developer takes reasonable steps to secure data in transit and at rest when cloud sync is used (e.g., HTTPS and Supabase authentication). No storage method is perfectly secure; avoid storing highly sensitive personal information in the app.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact the developer to request deletion.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change; substantial changes will be communicated via release notes or an in-app notice when practical.

9. Additional notes

This privacy policy describes common Sidequest configurations. If you are using a build modified by a third party (custom server, analytics, or hosting), review that build’s specific privacy disclosures.
