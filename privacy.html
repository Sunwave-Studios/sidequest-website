Privacy Policy — Sidequest

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Summary

  • Sidequest requires an account and stores all user data in a cloud backend operated by the developer.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

1. Controller and contact

Developer: Jax Gardner
Contact for privacy/data requests: parkerjrox@gmail.com

2. What data we collect and store

All data is stored in the developer’s cloud backend and associated with your account. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Account identifiers and authentication information (email where provided).
  • Profile information you submit (display name and related profile fields).
  • App content and usage data: quest completions, saved quests, group membership, preferences, and other content you create or submit through the app.
  • Notification preferences and scheduling metadata required to deliver reminders.

3. How we use your data

Data is used to provide, operate, and improve the app, including account management, syncing across devices, group features, and notifications. We may use aggregated or anonymized data for internal analytics to improve the service; such aggregates do not identify individual users.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the official app builds beyond minimal server-side analytics used to run the service. Any third-party services used to host or operate the backend are governed by their own policies. The app may rely on OS-level services (for example, push notification delivery); those services are governed by the OS vendor.

5. Retention and deletion

Your account data is retained on the developer’s servers for as long as the account exists or as required to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated data via the app’s account deletion feature or by contacting the developer at parkerjrox@gmail.com. Server-side deletions may take effect after backend processing and are irreversible.

6. Security

The developer takes commercially reasonable steps to protect data in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure; avoid storing highly sensitive personal information in the app.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact the developer to request deletion.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change; substantial changes will be communicated via release notes or an in-app notice when practical.

9. Additional notes

This policy describes the official builds of Sidequest provided by the developer. Third-party builds or forks may operate differently; review their specific disclosures before use.
